# Register: correspondence (example pack)

**Fictional example.** The message below is invented. A real register file may quote the author's own sent email, with permission, recipients and third-party names removed, and only because the pack stays private.

Email and messages. Tier 1 by default; tier 2 when it matters (a client escalation, a decision others will rely on).

## Exemplar: replying to a delayed-works query

Fictional sent email, 11 June 2026 · unassisted · verbatim, 98 words

> Thanks for flagging this. Short version: the resurfacing moves to the week of 7 July, because the drainage survey found a collapsed section under the eastbound lane and we're not laying new surface over it.
>
> What that means for you:
> - the lane closure on the 23rd is cancelled;
> - the July closure is the same length, same hours;
> - the drainage repair itself is overnight, so no daytime impact.
>
> I'll confirm the July dates by Friday once the repair is programmed. If the 7th clashes with your deliveries, tell me now and we'll look at the following week.
>
> Thanks, J.

## Patterns

1. **Opens by placing the reader**, often "Thanks for…" or "Short version:", then the answer.
2. **The reason comes with the change**, in the same sentence.
3. **Lists for what the reader must do or expect**, punctuated as a sentence.
4. **A dated next step**, and who takes it.
5. **Signs off with an initial.**

## What never goes in

- Apologies for things that weren't anyone's fault.
- "I hope this email finds you well" or any opener that delays the answer.
- Commitments the author hasn't confirmed they can keep.
