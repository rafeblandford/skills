# Register: short post (example pack)

**Fictional example.** The passages below are invented. A real register file quotes the author verbatim, with source, date, provenance and word count.

Short public prose: a professional-network post, a note on a project page, an announcement. Tier 2 by default. Read with `profile.md` and `craft.md`.

## Exemplar 1: a trial result

Fictional post, 21 May 2026 · unassisted · verbatim, 70 words

> We moved six culvert inspections from February to August last year. Four of them found scour the winter inspections had missed; two found nothing new. Six is not many, and I'm not claiming more than that. But the cost was one extra week of a second crew, and the alternative is a collapsed headwall. I've written up what we did and what I'd change, for anyone running a similar framework.

## Exemplar 2: crediting a team

> The bearing replacement on the river viaduct finished three days early, and that is down to Tom Okafor's sequencing, not luck. He moved the jacking to night possessions so the deck never carried full traffic on temporary supports. It cost us two extra night shifts and saved a week of lane closures. Good call, well made.

## Register notes

1. **One finding per post**, stated in the first sentence.
2. **The denominator travels with the number.** "Four of six", never "most".
3. **Reader address is higher here than in long pieces** ("for anyone running…"), and it's recent: present, but don't add more.
4. **Credit comes with the specific decision**, not general praise.
5. **The trade-off is still named**, in one sentence.
6. **No hashtags, no closing question, no "thoughts?".**
