## Treatment

- Set the wordmark in the house-style neutral grotesk, semibold, with normal lowercase spacing. Do not invent a bespoke wordmark typeface.
- Preserve the cyan-to-blue colour variation inside the supplied mark.
- When a single flat brand accent is needed, use approximately `#3195BA`; keep the house-style yellow for editorial markers.
- Use charcoal wordmark text on light backgrounds and white only on a sufficiently dark field.
- Keep the combined mark and wordmark subordinate to the concept identity and proposition.

## Application

Apply the small mark-plus-wordmark signature to the editorial wrapper, presentation packaging and design-handoff record. Reserve a quiet wrapper edge or corner before laying out the artefact; do not place the signature over the visual field as an afterthought.

Do not insert the signature inside a neutral or third-party product mock-up. A product surface may use this brand only when the brief explicitly states that the depicted product is rafeblandford-branded.
