IDEA

Explore a neutral product idea for neighbours borrowing rarely used household or DIY items instead of buying them for one job.

The important mechanism is not merely matching. A borrower says what they need and when, a nearby owner chooses whether to offer it, both agree collection and return, and the product keeps the loop visible until the item is safely back.

Create:

1. a scanned proposition sketch-page; and
2. an editable mechanism line-board.

Use the same default house style across both. Make the proposition sketch communicate both the human hand-off and the distinctive product intervention. Make the mechanism board easy to understand and rearrange in a workshop.

Do not turn the idea into a marketplace or imply that identity, payment, insurance or liability has been solved.

The central question is:

Does a visible return commitment build trust—or make neighbourly borrowing feel too transactional?
