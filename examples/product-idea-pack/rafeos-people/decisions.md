# RafeOS People — accepted direction through Draft 2

## Preserve from the conversation

- One shared people memory supports two explicit outcomes: everyday agent context and proactive relationship support. Neither is an incidental feature of the other.
- A person can have overlapping professional and personal relationships with Rafe.
- The curated record is a concise starting point for agents, with routes into deeper source material. Markdown supports human inspection as well.
- Ordinary authorised activity supplies most updates. New people are proposed for human graduation, where Rafe adds meaning, corrections and relationship intention.
- Distinguish observed facts, user-reported information, passing mentions and interpretations. Provenance and confidence are separate concepts; a personal assessment stays attributed.
- Intention for a relationship belongs to Rafe. Do not infer it from message frequency.
- Information tiers control both access and permissible use. Restricted planning detail is excluded from a separate drafting context by default.
- Definite commitments link to the existing RafeOS task system. Relationship suggestions can remain suggestions.
- Cockpit carries everyday awareness. WhatsApp carries prompts with a reason for extra prominence. Codex or Claude Cowork can host a richer people review when Rafe has time.
- Review depth follows available attention. Relationships come first; curation can be woven into the conversation or deferred.
- Missing a review must not prevent supported updates or useful nudges. Returning should bring a freshly prioritised review.
- Responses such as “we met last weekend” must affect all surfaces through shared state.
- Anna Mercer and everyone depicted are entirely fictional, with no basis in a real person. The supplied screenshot is a visual reference only.

## Draft 1 editorial choices

- Working name: **RafeOS People**, a neutral label derived from the user's proposed module.
- Proposition: **Remember the context. Make time for people.**
- Three beats: **Gather useful context → Curate a trusted memory → Help at the right moment.**
- Discussion question: **When should RafeOS prompt you, and when should it wait?**
- Three formats, four boards: one proposition sketch, one mechanism board and two product-storyboard pages.
- Primary feedback target: the people-review storyboard. Everyday-context storyboard is a separate view of the other core outcome.
- Sketch emphasis is balanced: a human situation, a readable prompt on a phone, and a social outcome. The drawing is an independent bitmap; annotations and interface text are editable SVG.
- All static boards are 1920 × 1080 with the editorial-instrument@2 frame. The Cockpit fragment borrows host hierarchy and colour; assistant shells are neutral conceptual views.
- No interactive prototype is included because these drafts are intended to settle credibility and structure. A later prototype would test correction, deferral and reminder confirmation across shared state.

## Proposals still open

- Fortnightly human review, with more frequent background preparation.
- The number and ranking of candidates, review items and daily prompts.
- Classification of update types that can be applied automatically.
- Exact meaning of each access tier, connector coverage, direct-file restrictions and how an authorised planning assistant hands off to a drafting assistant without carrying restricted detail.
- The “2 suggestions · 1 record to clarify” review summary and “about 10 minutes” are interface examples, not limits or measured durations.

## Tools and reproducibility

- Built-in image_gen generated the illustration. Its exact prompt is saved beside the independent ink layer.
- Editable SVGs are generated by `assets/build_boards.py`, which uses Python's standard library.
- PNGs use the installed Sharp renderer through `assets/render_boards.cjs`. All assets are local and SVG images are embedded.
- A fresh visual review was requested under the skill's multi-format QA instruction. Final checks and corrections are recorded in `evaluation.md`.
- No source connector, existing RafeOS record, automation or messaging service was changed.


## Draft 2 — accepted changes and preserved invariants

- People prompts belong within the wider RafeOS briefing. Anna is one of several people worth considering, alongside ordinary task, message, transport and home items.
- The people summary opens a richer review. The review can show a prioritised subset: “2 of 3 catch-ups · 1 record to clarify”. The exact counts remain illustrative.
- Everyday assistance should be visibly situated in an existing AI assistant. Draft 2 uses a Codex-style mock-up with app chrome, a project/task sidebar, conversation, source activity and composer. It is explicitly a reconstruction, not an exact screen capture.
- The scoped people API is reusable infrastructure for multiple kinds of application. Preserve the two defined outcomes and add a dashed “Other uses to explore” branch with no invented committed features.
- Source hyphens align with the visual centre of their headings.
- The proposition sketch shifts from balanced to product-led emphasis at the user's request: more explanatory information, smaller human vignettes, a larger straight phone and restrained ink-colour phone copy. The image edit changes composition while preserving the drawing style and fictional people.
- Add a fifth board, the native product preview, to judge the experience “as if it had been created”. Its compact editorial frame sits outside the product UI. Standalone product images omit editorial metadata and are labelled by filenames and this guide.
- The preview shows different moments: Cockpit before review; Codex using the corrected memory later; WhatsApp providing an independent timely prompt. The birthday example uses Sam, an entirely fictional supporting person.
- Preserve the original proposition, three editorial beats, discussion question, primary people-review target, Anna's fictional story and all trust/agency rules.
