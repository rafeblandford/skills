# RafeOS People — starting point

This example did not begin with a prepared product brief. Rafe first asked to
discuss an idea without invoking Product Idea Pack, then supplied the following
rough description. The complete edited discussion is preserved in
[`conversation.md`](conversation.md).

> Add a people module to RafeOS. Previous personal CRM systems required too
> much manual maintenance. Instead, ordinary authorised activity—email,
> messaging, meetings and agent conversations—could help maintain concise
> Markdown people records with provenance, confidence and access controls.
>
> Agents could use the record to recognise somebody, retrieve the relevant
> history and work with better context. RafeOS could also help maintain
> relationships by surfacing birthdays, unfinished intentions and worthwhile
> reasons to reconnect. New people and consequential interpretations would
> require human graduation or correction.
>
> The important questions are how the memory is maintained, how sensitive
> information is scoped, where it appears in existing services and how the
> system remains useful without becoming another CRM chore.

After approximately 15 minutes of active discussion, Rafe explicitly said:

> We can use the skill now.

No real person was used as the example. Anna Mercer, Sam, their organisations,
messages, events and relationship history are fictional.
