## What this example demonstrates

RafeOS People is the lead conversation-led example. It begins with an unresolved
idea rather than a prepared brief, holds the skill until the human explicitly
invokes it, and carries accepted decisions into three visual formats. Human
feedback then changes product emphasis, host-product credibility and the
mechanism without discarding the original work.

The five final content boards are:

1. a product-led proposition sketch;
2. a mechanism board exposing curation, human judgement and scoped access;
3. an everyday-assistance product storyboard inside a recognisable AI-assistant
   surface;
4. a people-review product storyboard in which human correction updates shared
   memory;
5. a native-product preview across Cockpit, a Codex-style assistant and
   WhatsApp.

A generated cover packages those boards into a six-page PDF. The cover is a
supporting delivery asset, not a fifth Product Idea Pack format.

## Human contribution

The first discussion separated two outcomes—shared context for agents and
active relationship support—then established relationship intention, delivery
surfaces, the fictional Anna example and the question “When should RafeOS prompt
you, and when should it wait?”

After Draft 1, Rafe requested a wider Cockpit briefing, a recognisable assistant
surface, better mechanism alignment, an open API branch and a more product-led
sketch. A second intervention requested an “as if it had been created” native
product board. The later PDF, cover and Figma work packages accepted Draft 2; it
does not count as another concept revision.

## Observed working time

The wider release testing suggests approximately **15–20 minutes for a typical
generation or revision turn**, depending on image generation, rendering and
destination checks. This is an observation, not a performance guarantee.

In the recorded RafeOS People task, the three main production turns took about
17, 20 and 22 minutes—approximately one hour of agent runtime in total—after an
exploratory conversation and with human pauses between turns. Rafe estimates
that producing a comparable four- or five-board pack manually could take a
working day or longer. That comparison is an informed personal estimate, not a
controlled benchmark.

The useful gain is faster movement from discussion to a concrete object for
critique. It does not remove the need to inspect the result, challenge its
assumptions, refine the language and decide whether its density and framing fit
the intended audience.

## Verification and limitations

- Every final board was rendered at 1920 × 1080 and visually reviewed.
- The pack checker completed with zero warnings.
- The six-page PDF retained selectable text and vector diagrams; generated
  illustration and photography remained raster.
- Six boards and three product surfaces were imported into Figma with editable
  text and grouped artwork. Hosted font substitutions and minor missing
  decorative chevrons are disclosed in the full hand-off.
- The private Cockpit reference screenshot is excluded. All people,
  conversations, organisations and operational data in the example are
  fictional.
- No working integrations, exact native application reproduction, access
  enforcement, market demand or relationship outcomes are claimed.

## Why it is retained

This is the strongest end-to-end example in the initial public set because it
shows conversation, structured brief reconstruction, cross-format translation,
meaningful human correction and a verified destination hand-off. The artefacts
are useful evidence of the workflow, not a claim that the first generated
answer is finished product design.
