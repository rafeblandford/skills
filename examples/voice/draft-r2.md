# The Mill Lane footbridge reopening

We reopened the Mill Lane footbridge this morning, three weeks into a nine-week programme, which is a nice thing to be able to write.

The background: the bridge's timber deck had been flagged in the spring inspection for rot in four of the twenty-two joists. The original plan was to replace the whole deck, which meant closing the crossing for nine weeks and sending pedestrians on a **1.4 km** detour.

| | Planned | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Closure | nine weeks | three weeks |
| Detour | 1.4 km, for three weeks | none |

*The plan against what happened.*

[DIAGRAM: the bearing end, before and after]

The survey told a different story. Coring the four flagged joists showed the rot was confined to the bearing ends, where water had been sitting on the abutment shelf. Replacing the bearing ends solved the problem without touching the rest of the deck. The new `weep-hole` drainage stops it coming back.

The full report goes to the parish council at next month's meeting.

Credit goes to the site team, who did the coring in a single weekend this month. Thank you, all.

The school run is the point: parents asked us to have the crossing open before term, and it is.
